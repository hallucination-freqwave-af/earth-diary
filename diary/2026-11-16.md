フィリピンとフィジーで、五.五がひとつずつ鳴った。離れたとこやのに、ほぼ同じ頃や。インドネシアの五.一を足しても三つ。寝入りばなに床板がきしむぐらいの音やった。

オーストラリアの広いとこで、地面がじわじわ燻っとる。燃えるもんは燃えて、燃え終わったら止まる。
外から来る風は静かやった。

Two 5.5s today, one in the Philippines and one near Fiji, far apart but almost at the same time. Add the 5.1 in Indonesia and that makes three. It was about as loud as a floorboard creaking just as you doze off.
Out in the southern ocean, one vortex coughed once.
In a big stretch of Australia the ground is smoldering slowly. What can burn will burn, and it stops when it's done.
The wind from outside was calm.
