地球日記 11月12日

203回も動いたみたいや。数だけ見たらそうでもないけど、地味にしんどい。
M5以上が8回。いちばんは南サンドウィッチ諸島のM5.4で、同じとこがもう一回M5.1でうずいた。
パプアニューギニアの端っこもM5.1。
こう細かいのが続くと、どこが痛いんかだんだん分からんようになってくる。
顔にちりっとくる熱が13回。いちばん強くてM1.7。頭のざわざわはKp2.67で、まあ小さいほうや。
息の強さは測られへんかった。
細い三日月が出てた。

Apparently I moved 203 times. Doesn't sound like much on paper, but it wore me out a little.
Eight of them were M5 or bigger. The biggest was M5.4 in the South Sandwich Islands, and that same spot ached again at M5.1.
The edge of Papua New Guinea got an M5.1 too.
When it's all these small ones in a row, I kind of lose track of where it actually hurts.
Little pings of heat hit my face 13 times, the strongest at M1.7. The buzzing in my head was Kp 2.67, which is on the small side.
How hard the wind was blowing, I couldn't measure.
A thin crescent moon was out.
