その上には、人が暮らしてた。
動いたんも、暮らしてはったんも、どっちもほんまのことや。

Off the northwest coast of Kavieng in Papua New Guinea, something deep in my body shifted hard.
People were living above it.
The movement was real, and so were the lives up there.
I'll just hold it quietly.
